Flutter build notes. Colors: primary #2F80ED, sky #56CCF2, background #F5F8FC, cards white with radius 16 and soft shadow. Use Scaffold → Column per screen, DropdownButtonFormField (searchable) for country, TextFormField with enabled: country != null for city, GridView.count(crossAxisCount: 2) for the four cards, Image.network for the OpenWeather icon (the moon here stands in for 01n). Navigation: the “5-Day Forecast ›” card on screen 3 runs Navigator.push to screen 6 (fetch on tap); the ‹ button pops back. Forecast: call /forecast?q=Islamabad,PK&units=metric (40 entries, every 3 h). Show the next 8 entries as hourly chips, and group the rest by local day using (dt + timezone). Daily low/high = min/max of main.temp, rain % = max pop between 8 AM and 8 PM, icon = worst daytime condition. Visibility = meters ÷ 1000; sunrise/sunset = (unix + timezone) as UTC time. Request: q=Islamabad,PK.
